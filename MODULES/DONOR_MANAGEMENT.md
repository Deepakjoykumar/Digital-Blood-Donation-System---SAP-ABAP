# Donor Management Module

Maintain donor profile records including donor ID, blood group, city, contact details, last donation date and an availability flag.

ABAP report: `ZBD_DONOR_REPORT`  
Database table: `ZBD_DONOR`

The availability flag is administrative information only. Confirm donor eligibility, consent and medical suitability through qualified clinical staff; do not use this sample report to determine eligibility.
